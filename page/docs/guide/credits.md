# 致谢

## 开源库

Pure Music 依赖以下开源库：

**音频** — BASS · BASS_FX · BASS WASAPI · 各类音频格式插件

**Rust** — lofty · rusqlite · flutter_rust_bridge

**Flutter / Dart** — provider · go_router · window_manager · hotkey_manager · dio · file_picker · sqlite3 · flutter_animate · flex_color_picker · material_symbols_icons · flutter_volume_controller · flutter_single_instance · pinyin · logger · xml · mesh_gradient · palette_generator · fl_charset

**图标** — [Silicon7921](https://ray.so/icon)
**字体** — [MiSans VF](https://hyperos.mi.com/font/zh/)
